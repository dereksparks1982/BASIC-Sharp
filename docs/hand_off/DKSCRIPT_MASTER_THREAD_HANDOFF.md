### Protected language decisions

- Body begins on the first content line: `[creature`, not a separate `[` line.
- `<then>` is a Connector, not the result itself.
- `<then>` and `<than>` are identical.
- `there` and `their` remain identical where the rule applies.
- `(damage` is an official word.
- Do not invent compound teaching terms such as `action target`.
- No duplicate language dictionary.
- No new official word without an explicit decision.
- The compiler and engine do the heavy lifting; the creator enjoys the ride.
