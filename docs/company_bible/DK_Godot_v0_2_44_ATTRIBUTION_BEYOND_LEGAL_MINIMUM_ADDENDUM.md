# DK Company Bible Addendum - v0.2.44 Attribution Beyond Legal Minimum

Date: 2026-07-04
Status: Mandatory

## Rule

DK credits creators even when the asset licence makes attribution optional.

Every externally sourced asset record should preserve the creator, original title, source, licence, acquisition date, and DK-specific edits or excerpt timing. CC0 and public-domain status do not erase creator credit.

The final project credits must also acknowledge development assistance with the wording:

> Development assistance provided with ChatGPT by OpenAI.

Do not falsely imply that a candidate asset, artist, musician, or company contributed to the active build when the asset was not actually imported or contracted.
