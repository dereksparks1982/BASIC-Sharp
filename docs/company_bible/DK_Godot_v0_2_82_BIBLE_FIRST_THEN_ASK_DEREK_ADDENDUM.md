# Company Bible Addendum - v0.2.82 Bible First, Then Ask Derek

**Date:** 2026-07-06  
**Status:** Mandatory  
**Scope:** All Demon Killer work

## Rule

When there is a question, uncertainty, ambiguity, or missing instruction during DK work:

1. Consult the active Company Bible first.
2. Consult the relevant current project records next, including Tapestry, system design, controls, changelogs, patch notes, and session logs where applicable.
3. Follow the documented answer when one exists.
4. Ask Derek only after confirming that the project records do not answer the question or that an owner-only decision is required.

Do not ask Derek to repeat documented information. Do not guess around missing answers before checking the records.

## Existing Approval Rules Remain Active

This rule does not bypass owner approvals. `scenes/Main.tscn` changes, Company Bible exceptions, and any other owner-reserved decisions still require the approvals already defined by the Company Bible.
