# BASIC# parser contract v0.1.33

Every Head is followed by `[` on its own line. Every instruction occupies its own line. `].` alone closes the Body, and internal instructions have no period. Current Heads are `KINDS`, `DEFINE`, `START`, `WHEN`, `IF`, `CONTROLS`, `HOVER`, and `CONTEXT`. Retired compact Bodies and `<then>/<than>` receive teaching diagnostics.
